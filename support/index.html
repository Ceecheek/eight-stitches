Support

Eight Stitches — cross-stitch patterns

Contact us. Send questions and bug reports to app.eight.stitches@gmail.com. We reply within a few days.

How do I restore Pro on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the app and tap Menu → Settings → Restore purchases. The same link is on the Pro screen.

How do I cancel the subscription?

Apple manages the subscription. In the app: Menu → Settings → Pro. Or: Settings on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions → Eight Stitches. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

How many patterns can I make for free?

Three patterns from photos and three from PDFs. Ready-made patterns from the catalog have no limit. Pro removes the limit and adds Gamma and Anchor threads and PDFs without a watermark.

Where are my patterns stored?

Only on your device. We do not see or keep them. Deleting the app removes your patterns and progress, so save the ones you need as PDF first. See the Privacy Policy for details.
